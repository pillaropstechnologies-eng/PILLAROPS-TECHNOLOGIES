— Company Profile —

About PillarOps Technologies

PillarOps Technologies Private Limited builds cloud-based HRMS, CRM, reporting, and accounting software specifically tailored for oil & gas operators and industrial enterprises.

Our Story

PillarOps Technologies Private Limited was founded to solve a problem energy companies know well: critical business functions — HR, sales, finance, compliance — are often scattered across disconnected tools that were never designed for the pace and complexity of oil and gas operations.

We build cloud-based software that brings these functions together, so operators, field teams, and finance departments work from the same accurate, real-time data — from wellhead meter readings to royalty distribution ledgers.

Our Mission

To give oil and gas companies of every size access to enterprise-grade, cloud-based operational software — without enterprise-grade complexity or cost.

Our Vision

To become the operating backbone for energy companies across the region, connecting field operations to financial and regulatory outcomes on a single platform.

— Core Differentiators —

What Sets Us Apart

Why oil and gas leaders trust PillarOps to run mission-critical workflows.

Industry-First Design

Every module is built around real upstream workflows: meter readings, well ownership splits, JIB billing, and regulatory formats.

Cloud & Mobile-Ready

Field teams capture data on mobile devices with offline sync; office and executive teams see verified numbers instantly.

Modular Platform

Start with one module (e.g., HRMS or Reporting) and expand smoothly into accounts, CRM, and AI as your operations grow.

Local Support, Global Standards

Dedicated implementation and engineering teams who deeply understand regional regulatory requirements and global best practices.
