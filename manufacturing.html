Operational Software for Manufacturing Plants

Minimize maintenance delays, standardize quality checklists, and verify safety compliance across shifts. Built for rugged frontline environments — 100% operational offline.

Ditch standard forms. Run dynamic digital workflows.

Standard templates keep teams slow. PillarOps gives operators a technical playbook. Frontline technicians log OEE, perform machine walks, and report issues instantly. If a reading goes out of spec, the system triggers alerts and notifies floor managers.

1

Capture Shop-Floor Data

Operator performs an hourly inspection walk and logs a temperature anomaly on Line B.

2

Automate Response

The system flags the anomaly, halts the workflow, and auto-dispatches an emergency work ticket to Maintenance.

Visualize OEE Live

The anomaly, the resolution time, and the maintenance history display on the Ops Manager's live dashboard.
