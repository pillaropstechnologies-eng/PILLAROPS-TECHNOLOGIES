— Intelligent Operational Capabilities —

7 Native AI Capabilities Across PillarOps

Built directly into the core platform data layer — no third-party integrations or manual data exports required.

Predictive Maintenance

Machine learning models flag downhole pumps, compressors, and pipeline valves likely to fail based on historical runtimes, pressure trends, and temperature logs.

AI Production Forecasting

Forecast well, battery, and field output using machine learning algorithms trained on historical flow rates, choke changes, and reservoir decline dynamics.

Anomaly Detection

Automatically detect abnormal spikes or drops in daily meter readings, casing pressures, or separator temperatures before they trigger HSE incidents or spills.

Smart Allocation Assistance

AI-driven mathematical verification on complex commingled allocation splits, catching ownership discrepancies and meter variances before statements reach partners.

Natural-Language Assistant

Ask questions about field logs and financial metrics in plain English: "Show wells with >10% decline this month" and get instant graphs and drafted reports.

Document Intelligence

Automate data extraction and cross-verification from scanned field run tickets, contractor invoices, regulatory PDF forms, and division order deeds.

Automated Insights & Multi-Channel Alerts

Proactive push notifications and SMS dispatches sent to operations superintendents, pumpers, and accounting leads when critical metrics deviate from statistical baselines.
