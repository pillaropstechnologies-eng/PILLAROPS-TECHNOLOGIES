— Upstream Operations Software —

Purpose-Built Software for Upstream Operations

Beyond core business software, PillarOps offers dedicated modules addressing the operational realities of oil and gas production — from the field sensor to the regulator's desk.

Schedule an Upstream Demo ← All Industry Solutions

Field Data Capture

Collect daily production volumes, pressures, temperatures, tank gauge measurements, and run tickets directly from meters or mobile pumper entries — online or offline.

  • Offline local storage & conflict-free background sync
  • Direct SCADA and flow computer automated ingestion
  • Mandatory photo capture, GPS stamp, and valve checks
Explore Field Data Workflows →

Production Allocation

Splits measured sales volumes and fuel/flare losses back to specific wells, commingled facilities, and working interest owners based on precise contractual splits.

  • Multi-tier battery and gathering system allocation algorithms
  • Mathematically auditable calculation trails for partners
  • Direct integration with JIB and revenue accounting ledgers
Explore Allocation Engine →

Regulatory Compliance

Generates standardized, audit-ready filings and environmental reports formatted to satisfy state, national, and federal energy conservation authorities.

  • Automated statutory production and severance tax reporting
  • Flaring, venting, emissions, and water disposal audit tracking
  • Strict electronic records retention with immutable time stamps
Explore Compliance Tools →

Asset Performance Management

Tracks pumpjack uptime, compressor runtime, maintenance intervals, vibration codes, and operational reliability across geographically dispersed lease sites.

  • Real-time exception alerts and automatic dispatch triggers
  • AI predictive maintenance failure forecasts for high-value gear
  • Historical mean-time-between-failure (MTBF) analytics
Explore APM & AI Uptime →
